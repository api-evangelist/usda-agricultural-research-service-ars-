USDA researchers developed an indoor air scrubber that reduces ammonia levels in chicken houses by 87-99%. The device purifies air in a 40x400-foot facility every 30 minutes and features a fast sand filter to prevent clogging, addressing a problem that affects both poultry health and worker safety.
