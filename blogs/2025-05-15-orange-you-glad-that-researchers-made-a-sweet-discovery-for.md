Orange You Glad that Researchers Made a “Sweet” Discovery for the Citrus Industry? By: Jessica Ryan Email: Jessica.Ryan@usda.gov ARS scientists made a “sweet” discovery that may be important to solve a major problem within the citrus industry. Huanglongbing (HLB), also known as citrus greening disease, poses a serious threat to the Florida citrus industry.
